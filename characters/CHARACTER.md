# Characters

The storybook is told as Pingfan's story, in the spirit of Rachel Coxcoon's
dissertation storybook: one main character whom we
follow through the research, with supporting people appearing where they fit.

Casting weight (how much story time each gets):
- **Pingfan Hu**, main character. The largest share of the story. He is the protagonist,
  and the story is told from his point of view as the lead researcher across the three
  studies. (Casting weight is a storytelling choice for this book; it is not a claim about
  anyone's exact share of the work.)
- **John Helveston**, secondary character. Second-largest share. The advisor and steady
  guiding presence across all three studies.
- **Everyone else**, supporting cast. Each appears once or twice, placed with the study
  they belong to. We do not detail or rank individual contributions; the storybook keeps it
  simple and collegial.

Portrait style: **Studio Ghibli** illustration, generated with `/ph-image` from each
person's real photo in `original/`, saved to `cartoon/`.

Wardrobe note: each portrait shows a *reference* look for the character, not a fixed
uniform. Clothing and accessories may change from spread to spread to fit the scene. We do
not need to keep anyone in a single outfit. For example, Pingfan need not always wear the
scarf from his portrait, and others need not always wear the jacket, cap, or shirt shown.
The "Look" lines below describe each person's defining, recognizable features (face, hair,
glasses, build) plus a reference outfit, but only the recognizable features must stay
constant.

Sources: each person's role and affiliation below is drawn from the project's own People
page (https://sc.pingfanhu.com/people) and verified against public profiles where titles
have changed. The "Look" lines are written from each person's real reference photo in
`original/`.

## Secondary character

### John Helveston
- **Who:** Pingfan's advisor and the project's Principal Investigator. Associate Professor
  in the Department of Engineering Management and Systems Engineering, George Washington
  University. PhD in Engineering and Public Policy, Carnegie Mellon University.
  ORCID 0000-0002-2657-9191.
- **Research:** Technological change and the transition to sustainable, energy-saving
  technologies, with expertise in discrete choice modeling and conjoint analysis; a noted
  expert on electric vehicles and China's EV industry.
- **Role in the story:** The mentor and constant backup across every study. Unusually, he
  found Pingfan first and sent the email that started it all; their first chat ran three
  hours. He points the way without taking the wheel.
- **Photo:** `characters/original/john-helveston.png` -> **portrait:** `characters/cartoon/john-helveston.png`
- **Look:** Fit white man, about 38, with a lean angular face and prominent cheekbones, a
  closely buzzed and receding hairline with very short grayish-blond hair, light stubble,
  and warm hazel eyes; navy blazer over a navy polka-dot shirt, no glasses; warm slight
  smile. Youthful and energetic, never elderly.
